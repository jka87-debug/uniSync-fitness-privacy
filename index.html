Last updated: December 2025

2. Health & Fitness Data

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

You may choose not to provide certain information, but some features of the App may not function properly as a result.

Children’s Privacy

UniSync Fitness is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

Contact Us
